Utilities and Energy Outsourcing in the Philippines

Energy customer service outsourcing philippines teams from ProVizion take the billing calls, connection requests and outage enquiries that flood a utility's contact centre, and handle them with the care that regulated customers deserve. Billing, meter and account enquiries handled properly

Most utility contacts are about the bill. Why is it higher than last quarter, why was an estimated read used, can the due date move. Agents who cannot read a bill confidently make these calls longer and angrier.

Our energy teams are trained on your tariff structures, daily supply charges, controlled load and solar feed-in arrangements, and on reading interval and basic meter data. They can raise a re-read, apply a concession, correct a mis-billed transfer and update contact details within the permissions you set. Complex rebilling goes to your senior billing analysts with a clean case summary attached. Where a utility runs a large data correction backlog alongside customer contact, we staff that separately as back office processing.

Move-ins, move-outs and connection requests

Moving house is when most people actually speak to their energy or water provider, and it sets the tone for the relationship. Agents handle new connections, transfers, final reads and disconnections in your CRM, check identification and concession eligibility, confirm site access details for the network, and set expectations on connection timing.

For solar installers the equivalent workflow is the sales-to-installation handoff: confirming the design sign-off, scheduling the crew, coordinating the grid connection application with the distributor and following up on inspection outcomes. Teams work inside OpenSolar, SolarEdge and Enphase portals, ServiceM8 and your own job management tool. The same scheduling skills apply in the building sector, and our construction and trades outsourcing page describes that side of the work.

Hardship, payment plans and vulnerable customers

Hardship conversations are the most important calls in a utility business and the ones regulators watch most closely. A customer behind on their bill may be dealing with job loss or illness, and how the call is handled matters both ethically and under your retail code.

We recruit for empathy as well as accuracy for these queues, and train agents on your hardship programme, on the indicators of vulnerability, on what a customer must be offered before disconnection and on when to hand over to a specialist. Agents can set up payment plans, apply relief grants, note a medical dependency flag and pause collections, always within the rules you define, with every action recorded against the account.

Heatwaves, storms and outage information lines

A heatwave, a winter storm or a bushfire can push contact volume many times above normal within an hour. Customers want to know when supply will return and whether a relative on oxygen should be moved.

  • Cross-trained core staff move from billing onto the outage line as soon as an event is declared
  • Pre-agreed scripts cover known outage causes, estimated restoration messaging and life support customer priorities
  • Team leaders reallocate rosters and extend shifts within Philippine labour rules
  • For sustained events, programmes above a few dozen seats can draw on partner facilities, each with more than 1,000 seats

Manila's UTC+8 time zone lets a day shift cover Australia, an afternoon shift cover the UK and a night shift cover North America, so an event in any of those markets lands on a fully staffed floor.

Regulatory recording and building the energy team

Utilities operate under retail codes and ombudsman schemes, and every contact must be recorded in a way that survives an audit. Agents capture explicit informed consent for transfers, hardship conversations and complaint stages in your CRM, and QA scorecards are built around your regulatory obligations.

Building the team starts with mapping your contact types, your systems and your seasonal calendar. A shortlist is drawn from the 10,000-plus Filipino professionals in our network, the interviews and the final choice are yours, and the people you select are onboarded on your platforms and scripts. As standard, access is granted by role, activity is logged, and every hire is NDA-bound and background checked; where card payments are taken on the line, the engagement is built to satisfy PCI DSS. Everyone is employed full time, with health cover, paid leave and a route into QA and team lead roles, and many stay for years. Contract terms typically run three to twelve months depending on scale.

Frequently asked questions

Is energy customer service outsourcing philippines suitable for a regulated retailer with strict complaint handling rules?

Yes. Agents are trained on your retail code obligations, consent capture, complaint escalation stages and ombudsman processes, and QA is built around those requirements. Every interaction is logged in your CRM so your compliance team can evidence the full customer journey.

Can the team work in our billing platform such as Gentrack, Hansen or SAP IS-U?

Yes. Staff log in to your billing engine, CRM and meter data tools under your permissions. Nothing leaves your environment, and where payments are processed during calls the engagement is set up to satisfy PCI DSS.

How do you handle sensitive hardship and payment difficulty conversations?

We recruit for empathy and train agents on your hardship programme, vulnerability indicators and disconnection safeguards. They can set up payment plans, apply relief grants and pause collections within your rules, with anything outside those rules handed to your specialists.

What happens to staffing during a heatwave or storm outage event?

Core staff are cross-trained to move onto the outage line, team leaders extend and reallocate shifts, and pre-agreed scripts cover restoration messaging and life support priorities. Larger programmes can call on partner facilities for surges that last more than a day or two.

Can a solar installer use a small team for scheduling and installation coordination?

Yes. Many installers start with two or three staff handling lead follow-up, site assessment bookings, crew scheduling, grid connection paperwork and post-install monitoring alerts in tools such as OpenSolar and ServiceM8. A team that size can be live within 48 hours.
