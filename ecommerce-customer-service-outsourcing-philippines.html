Ecommerce Customer Service Outsourcing in the Philippines

Taming WISMO: the enquiry that fills every ecommerce inbox

Where-is-my-order messages are the single largest ticket category for most online stores, and they are the easiest to get wrong by answering slowly or vaguely. A well-run team resolves them in one reply: checking the order in Shopify or your OMS, pulling the carrier tracking event, explaining what the status actually means and giving a realistic next update. Agents are trained on your carriers' tracking conventions, your dispatch cut-offs and the exceptions that need a proactive message, such as a parcel stuck at a depot.

We also work with you to reduce WISMO at source, through clearer delivery estimates and a self-serve tracking link the team can point to.

Returns, refunds and applying policy with judgement

Your returns policy is a rulebook, but customers arrive with situations the rulebook did not anticipate. Agents are trained on the policy itself, on the exceptions you are willing to make, and on the escalation point for anything beyond that. Refunds, exchanges, store credit and partial refunds for damaged items are processed directly in Shopify, WooCommerce or your returns app, so the customer gets a resolution in the same conversation.

  • Return authorisations issued against your eligibility rules and window.
  • Photo evidence requested and reviewed for damage or wrong-item claims.
  • Fraud patterns flagged for your review rather than approved by default.
  • Refund status confirmed back to the customer once the payment gateway completes it.

Preventing chargebacks with speed and documentation

Most chargebacks start as an unanswered message. A customer who cannot get a reply about a missing parcel goes to their card issuer instead, and by then you are defending an order you would happily have refunded. Fast, complete responses across chat, email and social prevent the majority of disputes before they open. For those that do open, the team gathers the evidence your gateway asks for, including tracking proof, correspondence history and policy acceptance, and prepares the response for your approval.

Written channels are where this speed matters most, and we cover response-time targets in depth on our live chat outsourcing page.

Staffing for Black Friday, Christmas and EOFY

Peak trading is where an outsourced ecommerce team earns its place. Volume during Black Friday week, the run-up to Christmas and Australia's end of financial year sales can multiply within days, and a thin team simply cannot keep up. We plan peaks with you months ahead: forecasting volume from last year's orders and your promotional calendar, recruiting and training a surge group that joins the roster for the window, and pre-writing the macros and articles for the questions a sale generates.

Because Manila is UTC+8, day shifts cover Australia and New Zealand while night shifts cover North America. After the peak, the surge group rolls off and the core team stays.

Working in Gorgias, Zendesk and Shopify Inbox

Agents work in your helpdesk with your integrations, not a separate system. Gorgias is the most common platform among our retail clients because of its Shopify order view and macro variables; Zendesk is common for larger multi-brand groups; Shopify Inbox suits smaller stores that want chat close to the storefront. Many of our agents have used all three, along with Re:amaze, Freshdesk and the Amazon and eBay messaging consoles.

Keeping the helpdesk under your account means order history, customer tags, macros and reporting stay yours, and the team can be scaled in either direction without migrating anything. Reviews, social comments and the public side of service

Product reviews, marketplace feedback, Instagram comments and Facebook messages all shape whether the next shopper buys. The team monitors and responds to these channels under guidelines you set, thanks happy customers, resolves complaints by moving them to a private channel, and flags reputational issues to you the same day. Marketplace seller metrics, including Amazon's response-time and defect rate requirements, are tracked so your account health stays in good standing.

How ecommerce customer service outsourcing in the Philippines drives retention

Winning a new customer takes real effort; keeping one is a matter of how they were treated after they bought. A dedicated team learns your products, your bestsellers and your regulars, and can recognise a repeat buyer in the helpdesk before they say a word. That makes it possible to offer a replacement instead of a refund, suggest the right size on an exchange or add a note that a VIP had a bad delivery experience.

Agents are full-time ProVizion employees with health cover, paid leave and paths into QA and team lead roles, so the people who know your customers stay. You can run the team yourself or have a ProVizion team leader manage it, and change that later. Frequently asked questions

What order volume justifies ecommerce customer service outsourcing in the Philippines?

If your founders or marketing staff are answering tickets, or your reply times slip during promotions, a single dedicated agent is usually worthwhile. Teams of one to three people are common for growing stores and can be expanded as volume grows.

Can the team process refunds and exchanges without asking us each time?

Yes, within limits you define. Most clients set thresholds by order value and refund type that the team can approve directly, with anything above that escalated to you. The limits can be widened as trust builds.

How far in advance should we plan for peak season staffing?

For a large surge, start planning two to three months before the peak so recruitment, training and helpdesk access are complete beforehand. Smaller increases of a few seats can be arranged within weeks using our screened bench.

Do you cover Amazon and eBay messaging as well as our own store?

Yes. Agents work in the marketplace messaging consoles or through your helpdesk integration, and they are trained on each marketplace's response-time rules and policy language so your seller metrics are protected.

Will the team respond to product reviews and social media comments?

They can, using response guidelines you approve. Positive reviews receive a thank-you, complaints are acknowledged publicly and moved to a private channel for resolution, and anything sensitive is escalated to you before a public reply is posted.
