Sales Development Representative Jobs in the Philippines

ProVizion is hiring sales development representatives in the Philippines to open conversations for businesses in the United States, Australia and the United Kingdom. You will prospect by phone, email and LinkedIn, qualify interest and book meetings for your client's account executives.

This is a target-driven role for confident communicators who enjoy the pace of outbound sales. Positions are full time with HMO, paid leave and performance incentives on many accounts, and they are a strong first step toward account management and sales leadership.

What you will do

  • Make outbound calls and send personalised emails and LinkedIn messages to target prospects
  • Qualify interest against the client's criteria and book meetings for their sales team
  • Handle objections professionally and follow up until there is a clear answer
  • Log every activity and outcome accurately in the CRM
  • Research accounts and contacts before reaching out
  • Work toward daily activity targets and monthly meeting goals

"I can tell they have our best interest at heart because they're always going the extra mile for us."

What we are looking for

  • Fluent, confident spoken English with a clear and natural accent
  • Previous outbound sales, appointment setting or telesales experience is preferred
  • Resilience and a positive attitude when a prospect says no
  • Curiosity about the industries you will be selling into
  • Availability for night or mid shifts to match US and UK business hours

How SDRs are supported

You will not be handed a phone and a list on day one. Every SDR goes through training on the client's product, ideal customer and messaging, then practises calls with a coach before going live. Team leaders listen to calls each week and give specific feedback, and top performers move onto bigger accounts, senior SDR roles and team leadership.

Frequently asked questions

Are there incentives on top of the base salary?

On many accounts, yes. Incentives are usually tied to qualified meetings booked and are explained during your interview for the specific account you are being considered for.

I have customer service experience but not sales. Can I apply?

Yes. Many successful SDRs come from customer service. If you are comfortable starting conversations with strangers and can handle rejection without losing energy, apply and tell us about it.
