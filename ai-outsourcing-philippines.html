Two jobs AI companies keep handing us

AI businesses tend to run into the same two bottlenecks. The product grows faster than the team that supports it, so founders and engineers end up answering user questions, chasing failed payments and moderating the community. And the model needs a steady supply of human judgement, labelled data, rated outputs and reviewed edge cases, that a small in-house team cannot produce at the volume training and evaluation demand.

ProVizion builds teams for both. Some clients start with a support desk and later add an annotation pod; others begin with data work and move user support across once the model ships. Either way the people are full-time, trained on your product and guidelines, and work inside your tools.

User support for AI products

Supporting an AI product is different from supporting ordinary software. Users ask why the model gave a particular answer, report outputs they think are wrong, hit usage limits, question their credit balance and want help writing better prompts. Our agents learn how your product behaves, including its known limitations, so they can explain an unexpected result honestly rather than reading a script.

  • Ticket and chat support in Intercom, Zendesk, HubSpot or your own help centre
  • Billing, plan and usage-limit questions, with refunds handled under rules you set
  • Triage of bad-output reports, with examples tagged and passed to your model team
  • API and integration help for developer users, handled by technically trained agents

For products with a heavier technical queue, our technical support outsourcing teams follow the same tiered structure.

Data annotation and labelling

Annotation is where accuracy compounds. Our teams label text, images, audio and video to your guidelines: classification, entity tagging, bounding boxes and segmentation, transcription, sentiment and intent. Every project starts with a calibration batch scored against your gold-standard answers, and annotators move onto live work only once they reach the agreed accuracy.

Because the Philippines has a large English-speaking, university-educated workforce, we can usually recruit annotators with relevant backgrounds when a project needs them, such as nursing graduates for clinical text, accountants for financial documents or developers for code.

Model output evaluation and human feedback

Evaluation work asks people to judge what a model produced: rate responses against a rubric, compare two answers and pick the better one, flag factual errors or unsafe content, or write model responses that show what good looks like. This is the human feedback that fine-tuning and evaluation pipelines depend on, and it needs raters who read carefully and apply guidelines consistently.

We recruit raters for reading comprehension and judgement, run them through your rubric with worked examples, and track agreement between raters so drift is caught early. Team leaders hold regular calibration sessions where disputed items are discussed and the guideline is clarified, and those clarifications are shared with your research team.

Trust and safety and content moderation

Consumer AI products attract misuse, and the community around them needs looking after. Moderation teams review flagged prompts and outputs, enforce your usage policy, handle appeals and keep forums and Discord servers healthy. Escalation rules are agreed with your policy team in advance so difficult cases reach someone who can make the call.

Moderators are briefed on the content they will encounter before they start, rotate between queues and have access to wellbeing support. Looking after the people doing this work is part of keeping its quality high.

Onboarding, success and operations behind the product

As an AI company moves from early adopters to business customers, the work around the product grows: onboarding calls for new teams, usage reviews for larger accounts, renewal reminders, CRM upkeep, invoice follow-up and research for the sales team. Offshore staff take this on so your own people can stay focused on the model and the roadmap. Our SaaS customer support page covers the onboarding and retention side in more detail.

Staff work inside your systems rather than ours, with role-based access you control and can revoke. Every hire signs an NDA and clears a background check, and engagements that touch regulated or personal data can be set up to meet the requirements you are bound by, such as GDPR. Training data and model outputs stay in your environment.

Teams are drawn from a talent network of more than 10,000 professionals, hold full-time roles with health cover and paid leave, and can be managed by your own team or by a ProVizion team leader who runs rosters, QA and reporting. Engagements typically run three to twelve months, which suits both an ongoing support desk and a fixed annotation project.

Frequently asked questions

What does AI outsourcing Philippines teams from ProVizion actually cover?

Two kinds of work. The first is the human layer around an AI product: user support, onboarding, account help and community moderation. The second is work that feeds the model itself: data annotation and labelling, output evaluation and rating, prompt and response writing, and quality review of training data. Many clients start with one and add the other.

Can your annotators work in our own labelling platform?

Yes. Teams work in the tools you already use, whether that is Labelbox, Scale's platform, Label Studio, CVAT, an internal tool or a spreadsheet workflow. Access is role-based and stays inside your environment, so data does not move onto our systems.

How do you keep annotation quality consistent?

Every project starts with your guidelines and a calibration batch scored against gold-standard answers. Annotators who reach the agreed accuracy move onto live work, a share of each day's output is reviewed by a QA lead, and disagreements feed back into the guidelines. Accuracy and throughput are reported weekly.

Can the team handle sensitive or harmful content for trust and safety work?

Yes, with the right setup. Moderators are briefed on the content they will see before they start, rotate between queues to limit exposure, and have access to wellbeing support. We agree escalation rules with your policy team so edge cases reach a person who can decide them.

How quickly can an AI support or annotation team start?

A one to three person support desk or pilot annotation pod can often be working within 48 hours of candidates being chosen. Larger programmes, or projects that need specialist backgrounds such as coding, medicine or law, take a few weeks to recruit and calibrate.
