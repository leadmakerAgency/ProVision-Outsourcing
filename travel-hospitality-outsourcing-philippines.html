Travel and Hospitality Outsourcing in the Philippines

Travel customer service outsourcing philippines programmes from ProVizion are built for the businesses that live and die by the booking: online travel agencies, tour operators, hotel groups and short-term rental managers. Where travel customer service outsourcing philippines teams fit in your operation

Travel support is rarely one queue. An OTA has pre-booking sales chat, post-booking amendments and supplier disputes. A hotel group has central reservations, a loyalty desk and property-level guest messaging. A rental manager juggles Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com inboxes alongside cleaner scheduling.

We staff each of these as a distinct function with its own knowledge base and QA scorecard, rather than one general pool that knows a little about everything. Reservations agents are trained on fare rules and change fees; guest messaging agents on tone, response time targets and escalation paths for on-site issues. Many clients start with the queue that hurts most, often after-hours amendments, and expand from there into voice-led reservations support once the first team has proven itself.

GDS, PMS and channel manager work your agents can take on

Filipino travel agents are a deep pool. Manila has hosted airline, cruise and OTA support operations for well over a decade, so we can recruit people who already know their way around a PNR. Typical system work includes:

  • Amadeus and Sabre: PNR creation, reissues, involuntary rebooking during schedule changes, queue management and ticketing follow-ups
  • Opera and other PMS platforms: reservation entry, rate code checks, group block management, deposit and guarantee handling
  • Guesty, Hostaway and similar channel managers: unified inbox replies, calendar syncing, cleaning task assignment and review responses
  • Loyalty platforms: points queries, tier status disputes, missing stay credit and partner redemptions

Agents log in to your instances under their own credentials. Booking data, card details and guest profiles never leave your systems, and where payment handling is in scope the engagement can be built to satisfy PCI DSS.

Handling disruption days without a scramble

Every travel business knows the pattern: a typhoon or an airline IT failure lands on a Friday, and contact volume climbs steeply inside an hour. The queue fills with people who are stranded and anxious.

We design travel teams with disruption in mind. Core staff are cross-trained across amendments and cancellations so the whole team can swing onto rebooking. Team leaders keep a bench of recently trained agents who can be pulled from adjacent queues, and for larger programmes we can draw on partner facilities with more than 1,000 seats when a surge outlasts a weekend. Scripts for mass rebooking, refund-versus-credit conversations and duty-of-care obligations are agreed with you in advance, so nobody is improvising policy at midnight.

Round-the-clock guest messaging and chat

Guests message at check-in time in their own time zone, and OTA customers expect a reply on WhatsApp or in-app chat within minutes, not hours. Manila's UTC+8 position lets us cover Australian and New Zealand daytime on a normal day shift, the UK and Europe on a split or afternoon shift, and North America overnight, all from the same site.

Chat-led work suits Filipino agents particularly well because written English is strong and multi-conversation handling is a trained skill. We typically pair dedicated chat agents with a smaller voice team for the calls that genuinely need a person on the line: a family missing a connection, a guest locked out of a rental at 11pm, a corporate travel booker changing twelve itineraries at once.

Loyalty desks and high-value guest care

A loyalty member who has spent years earning elite status expects to be recognised, not put through a generic script. Hotel groups and airlines increasingly want a separate desk for their top tiers, staffed by agents with longer tenure and authority to resolve issues on first contact.

Our model supports this with full-time employment, health cover, paid leave and career progression into team lead, QA and training roles. Tenured staff stay, which matters when an agent's value lies in knowing your properties and your regulars. Setting up a reservations or guest support team with ProVizion

The process starts with a short scoping conversation about your queues, your systems and your peak calendar. From there we agree role profiles, shortlist candidates from our network of more than 10,000 Filipino professionals, and put forward people for you to interview. Frequently asked questions

Can travel customer service outsourcing philippines teams work directly in Amadeus, Sabre or Opera?

Yes. Agents log in to your own GDS and PMS instances under their own credentials, working PNRs, rate codes and group blocks within your permissions. We recruit from Manila's established pool of airline, cruise and OTA support staff, so many candidates arrive with hands-on experience already.

How do you cope with sudden disruption events such as storms or airline failures?

Teams are cross-trained across amendments and cancellations so everyone can move onto rebooking, and team leaders keep a bench of adjacent agents who can be redeployed. For extended surges, larger programmes can draw on partner facilities with more than 1,000 seats.

Can you cover guest messaging overnight for a US-based hotel or rental company?

Yes. Manila's night shift lines up with North American daytime, so a US or Canadian brand gets full business-hours coverage plus evenings. Australian and UK clients are covered on day and afternoon shifts respectively, which allows genuine 24/7 rosters from one site.

Do rental managers on Guesty or Hostaway need a large team to make this work?

No. Many short-term rental operators start with two or three agents handling the unified inbox, cleaning coordination and review replies. A team of that size can be live within 48 hours, and it grows as your property count does.

Who handles quality monitoring on reservation calls and chats?

That is your choice. You can run QA in-house with your own scorecards, or a ProVizion team leader can monitor calls and chats, run calibration sessions and coach agents against targets you set. Clients can switch between these arrangements as the programme develops.
