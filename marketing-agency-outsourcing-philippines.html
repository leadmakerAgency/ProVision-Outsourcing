Marketing Agency Outsourcing in the Philippines

ProVizion's agency outsourcing Philippines service gives marketing, digital and creative agencies a white-label operations team that keeps accounts running between the strategy meetings. Agency MVP, SEM/EQ and Keller Williams have used ProVizion for coordination, support and administrative work, and agencies of every size now use the same model to carry the reporting, scheduling and client-service load that eats into billable senior time.

This is a support and operations engagement rather than an outsourced marketing department. Your account managers, strategists and creatives keep the thinking; ProVizion coordinators handle the follow-through in your tools, from Asana, Monday or ClickUp for project tracking to Google Ads, Meta Business Suite, Sprout Social, Looker Studio and your reporting platform of choice. You direct them yourself or ask a ProVizion team leader to manage the roster and quality. A single coordinator can be onboarded within about two days, and an agency-wide pod covering several accounts is built over a few weeks.

Account coordination that keeps every client on schedule

Account managers spend a surprising share of the week chasing: assets from clients, approvals from stakeholders, tasks from the studio, answers from the media team. A ProVizion account coordinator owns that follow-up. They build and maintain the project plan in your PM tool, prepare meeting agendas and circulate notes with actions, track approvals, keep the content calendar current and warn the account lead when a deadline is drifting. The client still talks to your account manager; the account manager talks to someone who has already done the chasing.

Agencies that want the same person to support the founder or managing director with inbox and diary work often blend this role with a virtual assistant brief.

Monthly reporting produced before anyone has to ask

Reporting week should not stop the agency. ProVizion reporting analysts pull performance data from Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok, GA4, Search Console and your SEO tools, assemble the client's dashboard in Looker Studio, AgencyAnalytics or Databox, check it against last month for anomalies and prepare the commentary draft in your template for the strategist to refine. Ad hoc requests, such as a board pack or a campaign wrap-up, are turned around from the same data sources.

High-volume, rules-based tasks such as keyword list building, backlink audits and CRM list cleansing are handled by our data entry outsourcing teams so analysts are not tied up in spreadsheets.

Ad ops support for paid media teams

Paid media specialists should be optimising, not building. Our ad ops coordinators set up campaigns from the media plan, upload creative and copy variants, apply naming conventions and UTM parameters, configure conversion tracking and audiences, run QA checks before launch and monitor for disapprovals, budget pacing alerts and broken landing pages. They flag anomalies to the buyer rather than making bidding decisions themselves, which keeps strategy with your certified people.

  • Campaign builds in Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok and programmatic platforms
  • Pixel, tag and GTM implementation checks documented per client
  • Weekly pacing and spend reconciliation against the media plan

Community management and social scheduling under your brand

Social inboxes never close. ProVizion community managers moderate comments, respond to DMs in each client's approved tone, escalate complaints and sensitive topics by the rules you set, and schedule approved content through Sprout Social, Hootsuite, Later or Meta Business Suite. Because Manila's shifts cover Australian, US and UK hours, clients see replies in their own time zone rather than the next morning. Where a client needs real-time website conversations as well, our live chat outsourcing teams run the chat widget under the client's brand with the same escalation discipline.

Design, content and proposal production coordination

Creative work stalls on logistics. Our coordinators brief tasks into the studio from the account team's notes, manage revision rounds, keep asset libraries organised in Dropbox, Drive or your DAM, resize and export approved artwork to platform specs, upload blog posts and landing pages into WordPress, HubSpot or Webflow, and prepare proposal and pitch decks from your templates with case studies, scope tables and timelines already populated. Senior designers and writers spend their hours on the work only they can do.

Where a client's proposal pipeline is heavy, a dedicated proposal coordinator can manage the RFP calendar, compile past responses and track submission deadlines across the agency.

Client support desks and how agency outsourcing Philippines pods are managed

Agencies that manage websites, email platforms or marketing automation for clients end up running a help desk whether they planned to or not. ProVizion sets up a support desk in Zendesk, HubSpot or your ticketing tool for client requests such as content updates, form fixes, list uploads and campaign sends, with SLAs and a QA process so nothing falls between account management and the studio. Recruited from a talent pool of over 10,000 Filipino professionals, many with agency backgrounds, coordinators hold full-time positions that include health cover and paid leave, with promotion routes into team leadership, QA and training. Client platform access is permissioned per user and logged, every coordinator is NDA-bound and background checked, and client data stays in your systems. Agencies that grow beyond a handful of coordinators into a full offshore operations floor can find the detail on our dedicated teams page. A contract usually lasts anywhere from three months up to twelve.

Frequently asked questions

Is agency outsourcing Philippines the same as outsourcing our marketing services?

No. We do not sell marketing strategy, creative direction or media buying. We provide the coordination, reporting, ad ops, community management and support staff who carry out the operational work around your strategists and creatives, under your brand, inside your tools.

Will our clients know the work is done offshore?

Only if you tell them. Coordinators and community managers work from your email domain, your project management tool and your social scheduling platform, following the tone and escalation rules you set. Many agencies introduce them to clients as team members, which they are.

Can one coordinator support several client accounts?

Yes, and that is the common starting point. A coordinator typically supports a small portfolio of accounts under one or two account managers, with clear time allocation. As the portfolio grows, we add coordinators and, if you prefer, a ProVizion team leader to manage workload across the pod.

What platforms do your agency support staff know?

Asana, Monday, ClickUp and Trello for project management; Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn and TikTok for ad ops; Looker Studio, AgencyAnalytics and Databox for reporting; Sprout Social, Hootsuite and Later for social; WordPress, HubSpot and Webflow for content. Training on your specific setup happens before go-live.

How do you protect client accounts and ad platform access?

Staff are added as users with role-based permissions on each client's Business Manager, Google Ads MCC or CMS, never through shared logins. Activity is logged, each staff member has signed an NDA and been background checked, and access is removed immediately when a client or role changes.
