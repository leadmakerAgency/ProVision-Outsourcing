Medical Billing Outsourcing in the Philippines

Medical billing outsourcing philippines teams from ProVizion work the revenue cycle for US physician practices, specialty groups and third-party billing companies: charge entry, coding support, claim submission, denial management, accounts receivable follow-up, patient statements and payment posting. Since 2011 we have staffed dedicated Filipino teams for clients including USHEALTH Group, Allstate and Symmetry Financial Group, and healthcare revenue cycle work runs on the same foundation.

Your billers work inside your practice management and clearinghouse platforms, whether that is Kareo, AdvancedMD, eClinicalWorks, athenaOne, NextGen or Availity, under access you control. Nothing is exported to our own systems, and every engagement is structured to meet HIPAA requirements from the first day of access. You decide whether your billing manager runs the team or a ProVizion team leader handles rosters, quality and coaching.

Charge entry, coding support and claim scrubbing

Accuracy at charge entry determines everything downstream. Billers take encounter data from the EHR or superbill, enter charges with the correct CPT, modifiers and diagnosis pointers, and check the claim against payer edits before it leaves the practice management system.

Coding support is provided as a review and query function. Where your practice requires certified coders, we recruit staff holding CPC or equivalent credentials for that role; where a coding review is enough, experienced billers flag mismatches between documentation and codes for your provider to resolve. We do not assign codes that the documentation does not support, and we do not make clinical judgements. Claims that fail scrubbing are corrected and resubmitted the same day wherever possible.

Denial management and AR follow-up that actually gets worked

Denials pile up because nobody has time to work them. Our AR specialists are assigned to work queues by payer or by aging bucket and expected to touch every claim on a set cycle.

  • Denial categorisation by CARC and RARC code, with root cause fed back to charge entry and the front desk
  • Corrected claim and appeal preparation, including medical records requests and timely filing evidence
  • Payer portal and clearinghouse status checks in Availity, Change Healthcare, Waystar and similar tools
  • Outbound payer follow-up on claims with no response, with call reference numbers logged
  • Underpayment identification against contracted rates, escalated to your billing manager

The structured approach mirrors what we run for insurers on the other side of the transaction, described on our claims processing outsourcing page.

Payment posting, patient statements and balance enquiries

Electronic remittance advice needs to be posted quickly and correctly so AR reports are true. Our posters load ERAs, reconcile them against deposits, post manual EOBs and patient payments, apply contractual adjustments, transfer balances to secondary payers or patients, and flag variances.

Patient statements go out on your cycle, and patient balance enquiries are handled by the same team on an afternoon or night shift that lines up with US business hours, since Manila is UTC+8. Agents explain a statement line by line, set up payment plans within your policy, take card payments through your patient portal, and know when a balance question is really a coverage question for the front desk. Where a practice also wants its bookkeeping reconciled against billing deposits, our accounting outsourcing page describes that service.

HIPAA-structured access, security and audit trails

Protected health information never leaves your environment. Billers work through your PM system, clearinghouse and payer portals over secured connections, with role-based access limited to each person's functions. Activity is logged, and staff sign NDAs and complete background checks before they see a record.

We can structure engagements to meet HIPAA requirements, including business associate agreements, workforce training, minimum necessary access and incident reporting procedures, and we work with your compliance officer or your billing company's counsel to document the arrangement. HIPAA does not certify vendors, so we describe the controls in place and let your auditors test them.

Frequently asked questions

Is medical billing outsourcing philippines compatible with HIPAA obligations for a US practice?

Engagements can be structured to meet HIPAA requirements, with a business associate agreement, workforce training, role-based minimum necessary access, activity logging and documented incident procedures. PHI stays in your practice management and clearinghouse systems and is never copied to ours.

Can your billers work in Kareo, AdvancedMD or eClinicalWorks?

Yes. Teams work inside your existing PM, EHR and clearinghouse platforms under credentials you issue, and many of our billers have prior experience on those systems. Availity, Waystar, athenaOne and NextGen are also common on our teams.

Do you provide certified coders or only billing staff?

Both, depending on what your practice needs. Where certified coding is required we recruit staff holding CPC or equivalent credentials for that role. Where a coding review is sufficient, experienced billers flag documentation and code mismatches for your provider to resolve.

How is denial and AR follow-up work organised and measured?

Specialists are assigned work queues by payer or aging bucket and expected to touch every claim on a set cycle. Denials are categorised by CARC and RARC code, root causes are reported back, and performance is tracked on clean claim rate, days in AR and resolution volume.

Can a billing company use ProVizion staff across several practice clients?

Yes. Billing companies often build a team where each biller is assigned to specific practices for continuity, with cross-training for cover. Access is provisioned per practice so each client's data stays segregated inside its own PM system.
