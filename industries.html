Industries We Build Teams For

Looking at BPO Philippines by industry is useful because the same job title means something different in an insurance agency, a mortgage brokerage and a software company. A support agent for a home builder needs to understand site schedules; one for a SaaS product needs to understand user permissions. Recruiting for the sector, not just the role, is what makes an offshore hire productive in week two rather than month three.

ProVizion Outsourcing began in insurance and financial services in 2011 and has since built teams for the sectors below, for clients including USHEALTH Group, Anytime Fitness and Agency MVP. Each section describes the work that typically moves offshore in that industry and points to the page or service line that covers it in more depth. If your sector is not listed, that is a gap in the list rather than in our capability; describe the work and we will tell you honestly whether we have built a team like it.

Healthcare and medical billing

Patient scheduling, insurance verification, prior authorisations, claims submission, denial follow-up and billing enquiries for clinics, practices and billing companies, mainly in the US. We can structure engagements around HIPAA obligations, with role-based access, activity logging and signed agreements as standard. Our claims processing page covers the billing side.

Legal and accounting

Bookkeeping, accounts payable and receivable, reconciliations, tax preparation support, paralegal work, document review and matter administration for firms in Australia, the UK and North America. Staff work inside your practice management and accounting platforms, and confidentiality is protected through NDAs and background checks at hiring.

Logistics, travel and telecommunications

Booking and shipment tracking, exception handling, customer notifications, carrier liaison and account queries for freight forwarders, travel agencies and telco resellers. These sectors run around the clock, and Manila's UTC+8 position lets a single team cover Australian days and North American nights with a split shift for the UK. Voice-heavy work sits with our customer service teams.

Utilities, education and automotive

Billing and connection enquiries for energy and water retailers; admissions, enrolment and student support for education providers; service booking, parts enquiries and lead follow-up for dealerships and automotive groups. Each has predictable volumes and well-defined processes, which makes them straightforward to move offshore once the systems are accessible.

Nonprofit and membership organisations

Donor and member enquiries, database maintenance, event administration, grant paperwork and supporter communications for charities, associations and membership bodies that need to stretch limited budgets without cutting service. A small dedicated team often replaces a patchwork of volunteers and temporary staff.

Reading BPO Philippines by industry: what the sectors share

Across every group above, the teams that work best have three things in common: the work is done in systems we can access, someone on the client side can explain it, and the client chose deliberately between managing the team directly and having a ProVizion team leader run it. We employ our people full time, giving them health cover, paid leave and a career path, and that is why teams in regulated, detail-heavy sectors stay together long enough to become genuinely expert.

Frequently asked questions

Why organise BPO Philippines by industry rather than by service?

Because sector knowledge is what shortens the learning curve. A support agent who already understands insurance renewals or mortgage documentation is productive far sooner than a generalist, so we recruit against the industry as well as the role and train on your specific processes.

My industry is not on this list. Can you still help?

Probably. The list covers the sectors we work in most, not every sector we have served. Describe the work in terms of roles, hours, volumes and systems and we will tell you whether we have built a comparable team and how we would approach yours.

Do you have staff with industry licences or certifications?

For some sectors, such as insurance and medical billing, we recruit people with relevant background and prior experience with the same platforms. We do not claim certifications on your behalf; where a licence is required in your market, we work with you on how the role is structured around it.

Can a regulated business use an offshore team safely?

Yes, with the right structure. Engagements can be shaped around PCI DSS, HIPAA or GDPR obligations, staff never leave your systems, permissions are limited by role, activity is logged, and every person signs an NDA and passes background checks before starting.

Which industries move fastest from enquiry to a working team?

Recruitment, agencies, trades and ecommerce usually move quickest because the roles are well understood and the tools are cloud-based. Healthcare, insurance and legal take longer because compliance controls and platform access need to be arranged before onboarding begins.
