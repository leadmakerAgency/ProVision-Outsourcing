FAQ

Questions people ask before they book.

Not here? Book a call and ask us directly.

What kind of outsourcing company is ProVizion?

A Philippines-based provider of dedicated teams: customer service, technical support, back office, admin and offshore staffing. We don't do shared-pool call centre work.

How big does my team need to be?

One person is fine. Many clients start with a single role and grow from there.

Who manages the staff?

You can, directly, or a ProVizion team leader can. Either way you have a direct line to your team.

How fast can we start?

One to three person teams can be live in 48 hours. Larger, more complex teams take a few weeks.

What are the terms?

Typically three to twelve months depending on the scale of the agreement. We'll walk you through the options before you sign anything.
