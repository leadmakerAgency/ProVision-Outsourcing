Construction and Trades Outsourcing in the Philippines

ProVizion's construction admin outsourcing Philippines service gives trade businesses and builders in Australia and the US an office that runs while the owner is on the tools. We have delivered dedicated teams since 2011 for clients ranging from Anytime Fitness franchise operations to Keller Williams and ELR, and the trades version of that team handles the scheduling, quoting, ordering and invoicing that otherwise gets done at the kitchen table at ten at night.

Your admin staff work inside simPRO, ServiceTitan, Jobber or Buildertrend, answer the phone as your business, and talk to your suppliers, your customers and your techs in the field. You can manage them yourself over a morning call or have a ProVizion team leader supervise the roster and check the work. One person covering bookings and invoicing can be working within two days, and a multi-trade group or volume builder can grow the team into scheduling, estimating support and compliance as the workload demands.

Job scheduling and dispatch that keeps the vans moving

A plumbing or electrical business lives and dies by the board. ProVizion schedulers take inbound calls and web enquiries, qualify the job, book it against the right tech's skills and location, and keep the day tight so nobody drives across town for a twenty-minute call-out. When a job runs long or a tech calls in sick, they reshuffle the afternoon, notify the affected customers and update the schedule in ServiceTitan or simPRO so the field app reflects the change.

For HVAC businesses, maintenance agreements are scheduled ahead of the season, reminder calls go out in the shoulder months, and the summer and winter breakdown surge is planned with overflow capacity rather than voicemail.

Quoting from site notes, photos and voice memos

Most tradies are better at diagnosing a job than typing it up. Our estimating support staff take the site notes, photos and voice memos a tech sends from the job, build the quote in your software using your materials catalogue and labour templates, attach the terms and send it for the owner's sign-off or straight to the customer if you allow it. Follow-ups go out on a schedule until the customer accepts or declines, and accepted quotes are converted to jobs with materials lists ready for ordering.

Builders using Buildertrend get the same support on selections, variations and change orders, with the paperwork prepared for client signature and tracked against the contract sum.

Supplier orders, deliveries and stock on the van

Materials chasing is a full-time job on a busy site. ProVizion staff raise purchase orders with your wholesalers and merchants, check the supplier's figures against the quote, arrange delivery windows to site, chase back-orders and reconcile supplier invoices against what was actually delivered. Van stock lists are reviewed weekly and replenishment orders placed so techs are not running to the trade counter mid-job.

  • Purchase orders raised in simPRO or ServiceTitan and matched to jobs
  • Supplier invoices checked against POs before they reach your bookkeeper
  • Plant and equipment hire booked, extended and off-hired on time

Invoicing, progress claims and chasing what you are owed

Cash flow in the trades is a paperwork problem. Our staff invoice completed jobs the same day from the tech's job card, raise progress claims on larger projects to the agreed schedule, send statements and chase overdue accounts with polite persistence. They handle the back-and-forth with builders' accounts departments, respond to queries about variations and keep Xero, MYOB or QuickBooks in sync with the job management system. Owners who want the full accounts function offshore can extend the same team with offshore admin staff for bookkeeping support and payroll preparation.

Compliance documents, licences and safety paperwork

Nobody starts a trade business to write SWMS. ProVizion admin staff prepare safe work method statements and job safety analyses from your templates for each site, keep licence and insurance certificates current and on file for tenders and builder inductions, maintain the training and ticket register for your crew, and complete contractor onboarding portals such as Rapid Global or Cm3 that principal contractors demand. In the US, the equivalent work covers certificates of insurance, lien waivers, permit applications and OSHA training records. Property managers and strata bodies that depend on trades for maintenance can see how we support that side on our property management outsourcing page.

An after-hours booking line and why construction admin outsourcing Philippines fits the trades

Emergency calls at night and enquiries over the weekend go to whoever answers first. Because a Manila daytime roster matches Australian business hours and an overnight roster matches the US, a ProVizion booking line can answer as your business around the clock, triage true emergencies to the on-call tech and book everything else for the morning. Larger service companies run this as a small call centre with inbound and outbound lines; a sole trader might simply pair it with a virtual assistant who also handles email and social media enquiries. Staff are full-time employees drawn from a talent pool of more than 10,000 people, with health cover, paid leave and career progression, and they work under NDAs with logged, role-based access to your software. Engagements typically run three to twelve months.

Frequently asked questions

What does construction admin outsourcing Philippines include for a small plumbing or electrical business?

Usually one person covering phones, bookings, quoting from the tech's notes, supplier orders, invoicing and chasing payments, working inside your job management software. As the business grows, scheduling, estimating support and compliance paperwork can be split across a small team with a ProVizion team leader.

Can your staff answer the phone as our company?

Yes. Calls are answered in your business name using a script and tone you approve, on a number you provide or one we set up. Customers speak to someone who knows your service areas, your techs and the jobs you take on, and every call is logged as a job or enquiry in your software.

Which field service and building software have your teams used?

simPRO, ServiceTitan, Jobber, Buildertrend, Fergus, Tradify, AroFlo and ServiceM8, alongside Xero, MYOB and QuickBooks for accounts. Staff work in your licence with the permissions you set, so job data, customer records and financials stay in your systems.

How does after-hours emergency coverage work?

The Manila shift that matches your night or weekend answers the line, asks the triage questions you define, escalates genuine emergencies to your on-call tech by call or SMS, and books non-urgent jobs into the next available slot. You wake up to a full board rather than a voicemail queue.

Can they prepare quotes without a trade qualification?

They prepare the document, not the diagnosis. Working from the tech's site notes, photos and your catalogue, they build the quote in your template for review. Scope decisions and the final figure stay with you or your estimator, which is how most of our trade clients keep quality high and turnaround short.
